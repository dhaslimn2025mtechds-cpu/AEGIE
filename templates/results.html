Prototype Interpretation

AEGIE records real voice responses, transcribes them with Parakeet, extracts speech measurements, calculates MiniLM semantic evidence, passes the evidence through the agent workflow, and displays the results transparently.

The current DEVELOPMENT flow uses a fixed five-question sequence for reproducible software testing. Validated scoring and adaptive decisions remain calibration-gated.
