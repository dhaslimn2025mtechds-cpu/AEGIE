AEGIE ADAPTIVE INTERVIEW PROTOCOL
=================================

PROJECT:
AEGIE: A Research Framework for Adaptive Agentic AI-Based
Voice Interview Evaluation

TARGET DATASET:
6 IT job roles
300 real participants
10 genuine voice responses per participant
Total = 3,000 primary samples

INTERVIEW PROCEDURE:
1. Participant selects one of the six IT job roles.

2. Each participant answers 10 interview questions.

3. The interview begins with an Easy question.

4. After each response, AEGIE evaluates the response using
   the current adaptive evaluation mechanism.

5. The next question difficulty is selected according to
   the previous response performance.

ADAPTIVE RULE:

Low performance
-> Easy question

Moderate performance
-> Medium question

High performance
-> Hard question

6. A question already answered by the participant must
   not be repeated.

7. Question selection must remain within the participant's
   selected IT job role.

8. The interview stops after 10 genuine responses.

9. Audio, transcript and objective speech features are
   stored for research analysis.

10. Human annotators independently evaluate the collected
    responses using the predefined annotation rubric.

IMPORTANT RESEARCH SEPARATION:
Human annotation scores are research reference labels.

AEGIE predicted scores must be stored separately from
human annotation scores.

Human reference labels must not be used by AEGIE during
the live interview to choose the next question.

RESEARCH COMPARISON:
Baseline:
Static/non-adaptive interview evaluation.

Proposed:
AEGIE adaptive agentic interview evaluation.

The experimental study will compare the system outputs
against human reference annotations.
